# A toast is for what the operator has on

A toast is raised for a Held market, for Near liquidation, and for Liquidations. Nothing else toasts: not two spikes at once, not a Board move, not a Week or Quarter level. Those stay rows in the panel and in the record. A working order makes a market Held only while it is within 2% of filling. This replaces the toast line of [ADR 0013](0013-an-alert-is-a-fact-and-a-toast-is-a-coincidence.md); a row is still a fact.

The record for the 30 days to 6 Nov 2026 held about 6,500 rows, some 220 a day. No rule's rows were followed by a move with them more often than a coin flip: the share where Left beat Against ran from 0.35 to 0.51. The operator opened 3 to 7% of them. Board alone was 1,305 rows, every one a toast, at about 3.4 an hour, almost all between 0.5% and 0.6%. A toast that is wrong half the time and opened one time in twenty is noise, however true its fact.

What the operator acts on is what they have on. A Position moving, a liquidation price coming close, or an order about to fill is worth an interruption. A resting order far from the price is not: it made every spike on that market a toast.

The thresholds were raised with the record's own what-if, on the same 30 days. Board at 0.8% keeps 85 of 1,305 rows. A $250k volume floor keeps 464 of 2,259, and the share of those rows that were opened about doubles. Open interest at 3× keeps 539 of 1,205 and loses nothing in Left against Against.
