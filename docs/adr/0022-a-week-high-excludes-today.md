# A week high excludes today

A Week high, Week low, Quarter high or Quarter low is the extreme of the completed UTC days before today: 7 for the week, 90 for the quarter. The day being lived is not in it. A high printed today becomes the level tomorrow, once that day has closed.

Including today makes the level follow the price. Every market at its high of the day is then "at the week high," which is the same trap as calling the biggest minute of the hour a spike. A calendar quarter was the other candidate. It resets in January, April, July and October, and for the first weeks of a quarter it is a short window wearing a long name. Ninety completed days stays the same length every day.
