# Chart, Scan, and Watch share one panel

The chart panel has three modes: the price chart, Scan, and Watch. Scan and Watch are not mosaic tiles. Watch may undock to its own window; while it is out, the in-panel Watch unmounts so there is one Watch, not two.

A third mosaic panel would steal height from the price chart and the book. Scan and Watch are alternate uses of the same surface, not extra instruments. Mosaic would also imply they stay on screen with the chart; they do not.
