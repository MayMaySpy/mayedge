# MayRekt runs on the bars on screen

MayRekt colors the candles the price chart already shows: the active timeframe, the capped window, and the forming bar. A Pine runtime or a longer history fetch would match TradingView further left, and it would pull study machinery into the desk for a color. Tones on the left edge can disagree with TradingView until the lookbacks are warm. A closed bar does not change tone when later bars arrive.
