# The desk watches what the operator holds

The desk pushes when something about a held Position changes: no stop, a multiple of its risk reached, its Idea's stop closed through on the hour, its side crowded on funding, or funding eating its risk. Once a day it sends a brief of every Position. Most of these go once per Round trip, and the first look at a Round trip only seeds it, so a restart does not repeat what was already true.

The market alerts raise about 220 rows a day and say nothing about the operator's own book that a toast on a Held market does not. A swing trade needs few interruptions, and each should be about the trade: is it protected, is it working, is the reason for it still standing, and what does holding it cost. On 6 Nov 2026 seven Positions were open with no stop order among them, and nothing on the desk said so.

R is measured from the Idea's stop when there is one, because that is the risk the trade was planned on. Without an Idea it is the first stop seen in the Round trip, so a stop trailed into profit does not shrink R. The Idea's stop is judged on a closed hourly bar, not a print, so a wick through it is not news. The watch never acts on any of it ([ADR 0020](0020-an-idea-is-a-note-not-an-order.md)).
